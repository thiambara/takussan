# Switch

Switch (base-ui, `role="switch"`) of the logged-in area: a 24×40 track with a 20px thumb (on the ×0.9 scale on desktop).

- On: `bg-primary`. Off: `bg-controle-vide` (`--input` in light, 3.59:1 on cards; `--muted-foreground` in dark).
- The row is the target: wrap it in a `<label>` with its text; Space and Enter toggle it.

Source: `takussan-web/src/components/ui/switch.tsx`.
