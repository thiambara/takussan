# Popover

Floating panel anchored to a trigger, for filters and small forms.

- `PopoverContent` takes `side`, `align`, offsets and `collisionPadding`; `voile` adds a scrim behind it on small screens. `PopoverAnchor` positions it against another element.

Source: `takussan-web/src/components/ui/popover.tsx`.
